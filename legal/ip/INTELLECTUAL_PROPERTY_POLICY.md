## 2. Proprietary Restrictions & Usage Bounds

Except as explicitly authorized by Ujomor Systems under a formal, written Commercial License Agreement or EULA:
1. **No Reverse Engineering**: Licensees and third parties shall not decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer, or attempt to derive source code or underlying schemas from binary distributions of EAORCS.
2. **No Derivative Works**: No party may modify, adapt, translate, create derivative works, or extend EAORCS APIs or protocol specifications for external distribution without explicit authorization.
3. **No Benchmark Disclosure**: Public disclosure of performance benchmarks, security evaluations, or architectural comparisons without prior written consent from Ujomor Systems is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Third-Party & Open Source Software (OSS) Governance

EAORCS incorporates designated open-source components governed by their respective licenses (e.g., Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD-3-Clause). 

- **License Attribution**: A full bill of materials (SBOM) and open-source license attribution index is maintained in accordance with ISO/IEC 5230 (OpenChain) standards.
- **Copyleft Avoidance**: Ujomor Systems guarantees that no strong copyleft licensed components (e.g., GPLv3, AGPLv3) are statically linked into core proprietary runtime binaries, ensuring zero license contamination for enterprise integrators.
