# Commercial Subscription & Billing Policy

**Document ID:** `doc-com-01`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-05-16  
**Governance Authority:** Ujomor Systems Engineering & Governance Authority  
**Scope:** Global Commercial Subscriptions  

---

## 1. Subscription Structure & Billing Cycles

EAORCS is provided on an annual or multi-year subscription basis as defined in the commercial Order Form executed between Customer and **Ujomor Systems**. 

Subscriptions are billed in advance annually, with term commencement defined upon cryptographic license issuance.

---

## 2. Capacity Scaling & Overage Calculations

1. **Metered Operational Metrics**: License fees are calculated based on active node count, concurrent cluster instances, or total enterprise telemetry volume.
2. **True-Up Audits**: If operational metrics exceed contracted capacity during the subscription term by more than 5% for a continuous 14-day period, a pro-rata true-up invoice will be issued for the remaining term duration.

---

## 3. Payment Terms & Late Payment Remedies

- **Standard Payment Terms**: Net 30 days from date of invoice issuance.
- **Currency**: Payments must be remitted in the currency specified on the applicable Order Form (USD/EUR).
- **Late Payment Penalty**: Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.
- **Service Suspension**: Ujomor Systems reserves the right to suspend control plane updates and support services if payments exceed forty-five (45) days past due, following a 10-day cure warning.

---

## 4. Taxes & Regulatory Withholdings

Prices listed on Order Forms exclude applicable sales, value-added (VAT), use, or withholding taxes. Customer is responsible for remitting all applicable taxes imposed by taxing authorities, excluding taxes based on Ujomor Systems' net income.
