# Government Procurement Compliance Statement

**Document ID:** `doc-prc-01`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-05-16  
**Governance Authority:** Ujomor Systems Engineering & Governance Authority  
**Scope:** US / EU / Global Public Sector Procurement  

---

## 1. Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) Qualification

EAORCS qualifies as a "Commercial Item" / "Commercial Computer Software" under US Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR 2.101) and equivalent international public procurement frameworks (EU Directive 2014/24/EU).

Software is licensed to government entities under Ujomor Systems' standard commercial EULA (`doc-lic-01`) in accordance with FAR 12.212 and DFARS 227.7202.

---

## 2. Security Standards & Supply Chain Controls

### 2.1 NIST SP 800-171 & CMMC Compliance
EAORCS architecture aligns with NIST SP 800-171 controls and Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC Level 2/3) standards for protecting Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

### 2.2 DFARS Safeguarding & Cyber Incident Reporting
In compliance with DFARS 252.204-7012, Ujomor Systems maintains rapid incident response protocols and undertakes to report covered cyber incidents affecting government-contracted control infrastructure within 72 hours.

---

## 3. Trade Agreements Act (TAA) & Buy American Certification

EAORCS software development, cryptographic key construction, and core architecture engineering are conducted within qualified TAA-compliant designated countries.

---

## 4. Anti-Corruption & Ethical Operations

Ujomor Systems complies strictly with the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), UK Bribery Act, and OECD Convention on Combating Bribery of Foreign Public Officials. No gifts, bribes, or improper inducements are permitted in connection with government procurement actions.
