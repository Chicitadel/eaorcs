## 1. Ecosystem Domain & Service Topology (`*.airroofers.eu`)

All Air Roofers capabilities and services operate under unified `*.airroofers.eu` domain governance:

```text
                        AIR ROOFERS FEDERATED ECOSYSTEM (*.airroofers.eu)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                 UNIFIED PLATFORM GATEWAY                                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                          SHARED PLATFORM SERVICES (Infrastructure)                          │
│ • Mandatag  (`license.airroofers.eu`)  — Central Licensing & Entitlement Engine             │
│ • AeroBill  (`billing.airroofers.eu`)  — Central Subscriptions, Invoicing & Billing Engine │
│ • AirAuth   (`identity.airroofers.eu`) — Central Identity, SSO & WebAuthn Engine            │
│ • AirStream (`telemetry.airroofers.eu`)— Central Telemetry & Event Ingress Hub              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                         ECOSYSTEM CAPABILITIES (Commercial Products)                        │
│ • EAORCS    (`trust.airroofers.eu`)    — Software Trust & Autonomous Governance Capability   │
│ • CiviScore (`civiscore.airroofers.eu`)— Civic & Institutional Scoring Capability          │
│ • Akpati    (`akpati.airroofers.eu`)   — AI & Intelligence Processing Capability           │
│ • ConsuNexia(`consunexia.airroofers.eu`)— Consumer Relationship Management Capability        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Mandatag & AeroBill Service Roles:
- **Mandatag** (`license.airroofers.eu`) is the shared **Central Licensing & Entitlement Engine**, not an independent product.
- **AeroBill** (`billing.airroofers.eu`) is the shared **Central Subscriptions & Invoicing Engine**, not an independent product.
- EAORCS is hosted at `trust.airroofers.eu`, consuming Mandatag and AeroBill via Platform Adapters.
