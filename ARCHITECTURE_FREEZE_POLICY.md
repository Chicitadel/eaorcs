## 1. EXCEPTION-BASED ARCHITECTURE FREEZE DIRECTIVE

Core architectural domains for the **Enterprise Autonomous Operations & Release Control System (EAORCS)** are formally certified at **~99% completion** and are **FROZEN**.

> **Policy Directive**:  
> Core architectural domains are frozen. New top-level architectural domains require an Architecture Review Record (ARR), evidence that they cannot be implemented within existing contracts, and explicit approval by the Freeze Governance Board.

---

## 2. EXCEPTION PROCESS & ARCHITECTURE REVIEW RECORDS (ARR)

To introduce a new top-level architectural domain or constitutional law, a proposer MUST submit an Architecture Review Record (ARR) containing:

1. **Problem Statement & Business Rationale**
2. **Contract Impasse Evidence** (Proof that capability cannot be implemented within existing contracts)
3. **Execution & Determinism Impact Analysis**
4. **Backward Compatibility & Migration Strategy**
5. **Freeze Governance Board Review & Formal Approval**

---

## 3. PERMITTED OPERATIONAL WORKSTREAMS

All development MUST take place exclusively within the following 5 operational workstreams:

1. **Operational Qualification**: Continuous audit & benchmarking across real Air Roofers ecosystem projects.
2. **Performance Engineering**: Startup optimization, analysis latency reduction, memory management, and scaling.
3. **Commercialization & Licensing**: Installers, licensing keys, enterprise packaging, SDK bindings, documentation, and marketplace assets.
4. **Ecosystem Expansion**: Additional IDE extensions, CI/CD runners, OS platforms, and cloud connectors built strictly within existing contracts.
5. **Governance & Security Hardening**: Cryptographic signing, evidence integrity, vulnerability remediation, and LTS maintenance.
